# Roadmap

## Now — learn whether the workflow matters

- Walk through the internship search process with 8–10 students.
- Ask participants to show how they handled recent applications, without collecting personal documents.
- Record repeated friction: duplicate roles, lost job descriptions, forgotten follow-ups, resume-version confusion, or tracker abandonment.
- Let 5 students try this MVP with real roles and observe whether they return to it.
- Decide whether to keep, narrow, or stop based on repeated use and concrete workflow improvement.

## Next — only if the signal repeats

- Improve extraction using a curated sample of job descriptions and measure field accuracy.
- Add import/export that students already use (for example, CSV) if they request it.
- Test reminders in-app before considering email/calendar integration.
- Revisit the India-first segment after interviewing students from different campuses and job-search channels.

## Later — only with evidence

- Optional account and cross-device sync.
- Inbox parsing with explicit user consent and a clear data-retention policy.
- AI extraction if deterministic parsing fails often enough to justify model cost and privacy trade-offs.
- Institutional/placement-cell workflows only if a buyer and budget owner are identified.

## Not planned for v1

Auto-apply, job scraping at scale, mass outreach, resume rewriting, interview coaching, and autonomous agents. These increase risk and scope before the core capture-and-follow-up workflow is validated.
